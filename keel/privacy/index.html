Keel privacy policy

Effective 18 November 2026

The short version. Keel has no accounts and no server. What you log stays on your iPhone. Keel doesn't track you, show ads, use analytics, or sell or share data for marketing. A few features go online to look things up; each is listed below with exactly what it sends.

What Keel keeps on your iPhone

Your food log, weigh-ins and body measurements, workouts, goals, plans and settings. They are stored in Keel's own storage on your iPhone (and in a small file its widgets read). Like any app's data, they are part of your iPhone's backups (iCloud or your computer), held under your Apple Account. The developer never receives them.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Keel reads from Apple Health: steps; workouts, with heart rate and active energy during them; weight; body fat and lean body mass; and, only if you choose Apple Health as your food source, the food energy other apps log. Keel never writes to Apple Health. Health data is never used for advertising or marketing and is never sold. It only leaves your iPhone as described under "AI features", and only after you agree.

Camera, photos and location

  • Camera: to scan barcodes and photograph nutrition labels. Label photos are read on your iPhone and not sent anywhere.
  • Food photos: sent only if you use an AI photo estimate (see below).
  • Location: Ramadan mode asks for your approximate location once to work out suhoor and iftar times on your iPhone. It is not sent anywhere.

AI features

AI is optional and off until you add your own Anthropic API key. The key is kept in your iPhone's Keychain. Requests go straight from your iPhone to Anthropic under your own Anthropic account, and are handled under Anthropic's privacy policy and terms. The developer never receives them. What each feature sends:

  • Check-in reads and monthly reviews: the numbers on the screen Keel shows you before asking (for example average intake, trend weight, rate of change, steps and targets). Keel asks first and you can turn this off.
  • Photo estimate: the food photo.
  • Home-cooked dishes: the dish and ingredient words you type.
  • AI workout plans: your days, session length, goal, any notes you type, the exercises your equipment allows and your tracked lifts.
  • "Ask when Keel can't find it": the words you typed, the food names, or the photo; never your weight, goals or health data. Off until you turn it on and confirm.

Some text is written by Apple Intelligence on your iPhone; that never leaves your iPhone.

Feedback

"Send feedback" and "Report this" open an email in the Mail app. Nothing is sent unless you send it. It includes the app and iOS version, the iPhone model and, for a problem report, the kind of error; never your food, weights or health data.

Your choices

  • See and keep your data: Settings → Export my data.
  • Stop AI: remove the key in Settings → AI & data sources → Advanced.
  • Stop Health access: the Settings app → Health → Data Access & Devices → Keel.
  • Delete everything: delete the Keel app. Because there is no server, nothing remains with the developer.

Age

Keel is rated 16+. It doesn't set calorie cuts for anyone under 18.

Canada and Quebec

Keel is offered in Canada. Under PIPEDA and Quebec's privacy law (Law 25), the person responsible for privacy is Hassan Chaudhry, reachable at hassanchaudhryapps@gmail.com. The services listed above are outside Quebec and Canada (the United States and France); information reaches them only when you use the features described. Keel makes no decisions about you by automated processing beyond its on-phone suggestions, which you choose whether to accept.

Not medical advice

Keel gives estimates, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before a big change in diet or training.
