Even Keel support

Questions, problems or ideas: email hassanchaudhryapps@gmail.com. In the app, Settings → Send feedback opens a filled-in email; nothing is sent unless you send it.

Do I need an AI key?

No. Even Keel works fully without one. An Anthropic API key, added in Settings → AI & data sources → Advanced, turns on photo estimates, home-cooked dishes and AI reads of your check-ins. It is stored in your iPhone's Keychain and used on your own Anthropic account.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone. Even Keel has no account and no server. See the privacy policy for the few things that go online.

How do I save or move my data?

Settings → Export my data writes a file you can keep or share. "Restore from a file" reads it back.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the Even Keel app. To stop Even Keel reading Apple Health, open the Settings app → Health → Data Access & Devices → Even Keel.

Even Keel says it won't set a calorie cut

Even Keel doesn't set calorie cuts for anyone under 18. You can still track food and train.

Even Keel gives estimates, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before a big change in diet or training.
